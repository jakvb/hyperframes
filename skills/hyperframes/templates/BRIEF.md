## Belief ladder

What the viewer thinks after each movement. No two rungs may be the same.

| Movement | After it, the viewer believes |
|---|---|
| Question | something does not add up |
| Old picture | that explanation seems to work |
| Crack | the old answer cannot be right |
| Rebuild | I can see why the new answer must be so |
| Test | it is true, and here is the proof |
| Consequence | I can explain it, including its limits |
